The Customers module keeps the registry of everyone the company does business with: customers and suppliers, which can be companies (with CNPJ) or individuals (with CPF). The same company can be both a customer and a supplier. Each one can have several contacts and several addresses, at least one for billing and one for delivery. Sales reps should only see and edit the customers assigned to them, while managers see all. Every customer has a credit limit, which only the finance team can change. A customer with overdue payments must be blocked from new orders until the situation is resolved. Since we store personal data, we must follow LGPD, including handling requests from people who ask for their data to be removed.
