The Purchasing module handles how the company buys goods. Any department can create a purchase request describing what it needs; a buyer reviews it and decides what to buy. For purchases above a certain value, the buyer must collect quotations from at least three suppliers before choosing one. The chosen quotation becomes a purchase order, and orders above a value limit need approval from the purchasing manager before being sent. When goods arrive at a warehouse, the staff registers the receipt, which can be partial; differences between what was ordered and what was received must be recorded. Received goods must enter the stock automatically. Buyers should be able to see which orders are late.
