The Audit module is the central record of what happens in the ERP. Every relevant business action in any module, such as creating, changing, approving, or cancelling something, must be recorded with who did it, when, and what changed, including the values before and after. These records can never be edited or deleted by anyone, including administrators. Auditors and administrators must be able to search the records by user, module, type of action, and period, and export the results. Access to customers' personal data must also be recorded, to comply with LGPD. Financial and audit records must be kept for at least five years.
