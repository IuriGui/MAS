The Catalog module manages the products the company sells and buys. Each product has a unique SKU, a description, a category (categories can have subcategories), and a unit of measure; many products are sold by the box but kept in stock by the unit, so conversions between units are needed. We keep the NCM code of each product because it will be required for invoices in the future. Prices are organized in price lists, for example wholesale, retail, and special lists for key customers, and a product can have different prices in each list. Price changes must keep the previous values for reference. Products can be deactivated but never deleted if they were already used in any order. Only the purchasing and commercial managers can change prices.
