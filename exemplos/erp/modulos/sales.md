The Sales module covers the work of our sales reps, from quote to delivery. A rep creates a quote for a customer using the customer's price list; quotes are valid for a limited number of days and can be converted into a sales order. Each rep has a maximum discount they can give, and larger discounts need approval from the sales manager. Before an order is approved, the system must check the customer's credit limit and whether the customer is blocked. Approved orders reserve the stock, and the warehouse then picks, packs, and ships them; an order can be delivered in more than one shipment. Orders can be cancelled before shipping, releasing the reserved stock. Reps want to see the status of their orders at any time.
