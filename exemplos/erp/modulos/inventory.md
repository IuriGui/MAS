The Inventory module controls the stock of each product in each of our two warehouses. Every change in stock must be recorded as a movement: goods received from suppliers, goods shipped to customers, transfers between warehouses, and adjustments. Adjustments, such as losses, damage, or count differences, need a reason and must be approved by a warehouse supervisor. When a sales order is approved, the quantity must be reserved so it is not sold twice, and the available stock is what is left after reservations. Stock can never go negative. We want an alert when a product falls below its minimum stock level in a warehouse. Periodically we do physical counts, and the system must help compare the counted quantity with the recorded one.
