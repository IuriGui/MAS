The Finance module manages the money that comes in and goes out. Shipped sales orders generate accounts receivable, which can be split into installments with due dates; received purchase orders generate accounts payable. The finance team registers payments received and payments made, including partial payments, and late payments from customers are charged interest and a fine. Customers with overdue installments should be reported to sales so new orders can be blocked. At the end of each month the team performs the monthly closing: after a month is closed, its records can no longer be changed, only corrected with new entries in the open month. Managers need a cash flow view showing what is expected to come in and go out in the next weeks. Financial records must be kept for at least five years.
